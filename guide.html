Travel Guide

Everything you need to know before visiting Sylhet

How to Get There?

  • From Dhaka: Flight (Osmani Airport), Train (Upaban, Parabat, Joyantika), Bus (Shyamoli, Green Line, Saudia)
  • From Chittagong: Train (Paharika, Udayan Express), Bus (Shyamoli, Saudia, Hanif)
  • Local Transport: CNG, Auto-rickshaw, and local buses are available for getting around.

Best Time to Visit

  • Winter (Nov–Feb): Pleasant weather, ideal for sightseeing.
  • Monsoon (Jun–Sep): Ratargul & Haor are at their best.
  • Summer (Mar–May): Hot but tea gardens are lush green.

Travel Tips

  • Carry sufficient cash — many places don't accept digital payments.
  • Wear a life jacket when boating in Ratargul or Haor areas.
  • Hire a local guide for better experience.
  • Don't litter — keep the natural beauty clean.
  • Avoid wandering alone in remote areas at night.
Pro Tip: Visit Jaflong early morning for the best view of the sunrise over the hills!

Packing Checklist

  • Essential medicines
  • Camera & power bank
  • Sneakers & light bag
  • Raincoat & umbrella (monsoon)
  • Life jacket (for wetlands)
  • Sunscreen & insect repellent
