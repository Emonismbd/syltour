Sylheti Cuisine

Discover the authentic flavors of Sylhet

🍖

Satkora Beef Curry

A signature Sylheti dish made with beef and the unique satkora fruit (a citrus fruit native to the region).
Best at: Panch Bhai Restaurant, Panshi Restaurant
🍚

Panta Bhat & Shutki Vorta

Fermented rice served with dried fish paste — a traditional Bengali breakfast, popular during festivals.
Best at: Local street vendors, traditional homes
☕

Special Tea from Tea Gardens

Freshly brewed tea straight from the lush tea gardens of Srimangal and surrounding areas.
Best at: Any tea stall near the gardens
🍛

Handesh (Sweet)

A traditional sweet made from rice flour and molasses, often enjoyed during Eid and special occasions.
Best at: Local sweet shops in Sylhet city
🐟

Nona Ilish (Salty Hilsa)

Hilsa fish preserved in salt — a favorite among locals, especially during the monsoon season.
Best at: Traditional Sylheti restaurants
🥘

Bamboo Chicken Curry

Chicken cooked inside bamboo tubes, giving it a smoky and earthy flavor — a tribal specialty.
Best at: Local eateries in Jaflong area
